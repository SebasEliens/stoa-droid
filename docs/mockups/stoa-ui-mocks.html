Stoa: talk to the Stoics, offline

Six first-pass screens for a fully on-device RAG app on the Fairphone (Gen. 6). Tap things: citations open their source, setup runs, and picking a philosopher changes who answers in the chat.

1. Welcome

Decision: one screen that makes a single promise (private, offline, cited) before asking for a 500 MB download. The privacy pill is the value proposition, not a footnote.

Skipped: a three-page swipe carousel. Users skip carousels; the dots hint there's more but the button is enough.

Compose: Scaffold, Button, a vector ImageVector hero. Android 12+ also gives you a system SplashScreen before this.

2. First-run download

Decision: ship the texts and a prebuilt index-able corpus inside the APK (a few MB), download only the models. Show three honest steps so a long wait feels like progress, not a hang.

Skipped: bundling the model in the APK (Play caps base APKs; Play Asset Delivery is an option later) and hiding the model choice entirely.

Android: WorkManager foreground download that survives the app closing, LinearProgressIndicator. Sizes are approximate.

3. Chat with citations

Decision: two visibly different voices. The model's paraphrase is plain sans text; anything verbatim from the corpus is a serif quote with a bronze source line. Small models invent quotes, so the UI only lets real retrieved text look like scripture. Tap a 1 chip.

Skipped: classic chat bubbles for the answer (long answers read better as a page), and hiding sources behind a menu.

Compose: LazyColumn, ModalBottomSheet, NavigationBar, AssistChip. Tokens stream in via a Flow.

4. Choose a philosopher

Decision: the persona is just a retrieval filter plus a system prompt, so it's cheap to switch and lives in the chat's top bar, one tap away. Picking one here updates the chat screen.

Skipped: a separate chat thread per philosopher (more navigation), or an avatar gallery with generated portraits (no reliable likenesses, and it invites uncanny "AI Marcus" vibes).

Compose: selectable Cards with Modifier.selectable and a shared ViewModel holding the persona.

5. Library

Decision: the corpus is a first-class reader, not just hidden RAG fuel. "Read in context" from a citation lands here, and the same vector index powers search by meaning. Translations and years are shown because they're the reason the texts are free to ship.

Skipped: keyword-only search (embeddings are already on the phone, so semantic search is nearly free).

Compose: SearchBar, FilterChip, ListItem. Data from Room.

6. Settings

Decision: expose the RAG knobs a curious user (you) wants: passages per answer (top-k), tone (maps to temperature and length), and a storage breakdown, because 800 MB on a phone deserves an explanation.

Skipped: raw temperature and context-length sliders. Fun for us, confusing for everyone else; maybe a hidden developer screen.

Persistence: Jetpack DataStore. Sizes are illustrative.

Questions for you before we go further

Name and feel

"Stoa" (the porch where Zeno taught) with Aegean blue and marble. Keep it, or would you rather use Material You dynamic colour from your wallpaper?

Voice of the answer

Should Marcus speak in first person ("I wrote to myself…") or should a narrator explain what Marcus taught? First person is more fun; narrator is more honest.

Navigation

Three bottom tabs (Chat, Library, Settings) is the standard M3 pattern. Do you want chat history too, which would add a drawer or a fourth tab?

The "council" mode

"All three" mixes sources from every author. Should it answer once with mixed citations, or let each philosopher reply in turn like a small debate?

Default model

The 1B model is the safe default on a Fairphone 6. Worth offering the bigger one at all in v1, or keep one model and fewer decisions?

Daily touch

A morning passage notification or home-screen widget would suit the Stoic habit. In scope, or later?

Quotations use public-domain translations: Meditations (George Long, 1862), Enchiridion (T. W. Higginson, 1865), Letters to Lucilius (R. M. Gummere, 1917–25). Model sizes, token speed and passage counts are illustrative.
